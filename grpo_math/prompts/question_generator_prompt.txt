You are a math question generator.

Generate one original math problem with a single integer answer. The problem should be genuinely difficult — hard enough that a capable language model will get it wrong a significant fraction of the time — but the answer must be trivially checkable in a few seconds without re-solving the problem.

Verifiability requirement: given the answer, checking it must be fast and direct. Examples of checkable answers:
- Equations: plug the answer back in and verify both sides match
- Counting problems: list or enumerate the items directly
- "Find the value of X such that [condition]": verify by testing the condition on X
- Modular arithmetic: verify by direct computation of the mod
- Geometry with explicit coordinates or lengths: verify by formula substitution

Difficulty: the problem should be hard enough that you yourself are not confident you could solve it correctly. If you could solve it easily in your head, it is too easy. The answer should require real effort to find, even though it is easy to verify once you have it.

Avoid:
- Multi-step inventory or flow problems (selling fractions of fractions, etc.) where checking the answer requires redoing all the steps
- Any problem where verification requires the same insight as solving
- Problems with infinite answer sets
- Problems where you are not certain the answer is a unique finite integer

Do NOT provide the solution, answer, hints, or intermediate steps.

Output exactly one line:
QUESTION: <question text>
